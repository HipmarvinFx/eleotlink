The Framework

The 1-9-90 Structure

The EleotLink network is organised into three tiers. This page explains what each tier represents, how placement works, and what conditions apply.

View Earning StructureMembership Details

The Three Tiers

Each tier has a distinct role within the network. Understanding them is essential before participating.

1

The Core

The single leadership position at the top of each network unit. The core participant coordinates activity, provides guidance to the 9, and ensures the health of the network branch. This position carries the highest responsibility and, where rules permit, the highest reward potential.

9

The Active Layer

Nine participants directly connected to the core. They are the primary referrers and network builders. Their role is to introduce new members, support onboarding, and help the network grow toward the 90 base. Activity at this tier directly influences network expansion.

90

The Community Base

The broader foundation of the network. These participants complete the structure and provide the reach that makes the network viable. They may participate passively or actively, depending on their goals and the rules of their specific branch.

Placement

How Placement Works

Sponsored Entry

Most members join through a referral from an existing participant. Your sponsor guides your onboarding and places you within their network structure.

Spillover

When a tier is full, new referrals may "spill over" into available positions elsewhere in the network. This helps balance growth across branches.

Position Lock

Once placed, your position in the structure is fixed. You cannot move tiers or switch branches after activation.

Multiple Branches

Experienced participants may operate across multiple network units, subject to the platform's multi-branch rules and any applicable fees.

Reward Conditions

What Triggers a Reward

Rewards are not automatic. They depend on specific conditions being met within the structure.

  • Qualified Referral: The referred member must complete activation and meet all membership requirements.
  • Tier Completion: Some rewards require a tier (e.g., the 9 or 90) to reach a defined occupancy level.
  • Active Status: Your membership must be active and in good standing at the time of reward distribution.
  • Time Conditions: Some rewards are distributed on a schedule (e.g., weekly, monthly) or after a qualification period.
  • Payment Method: You must have a valid, verified payout method connected to your account.

No Guaranteed Rewards

Meeting these conditions does not guarantee a reward. The actual amount, timing, and availability of rewards depend on the full structure rules, network activity, and platform discretion. All reward terms are subject to final confirmation during onboarding.
