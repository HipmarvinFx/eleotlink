How to Read It

What Each Stage Represents

The visual is designed to make the member journey easy to understand without implying a guaranteed financial outcome.

Double X

Your starting build: two direct partner positions, with two next positions under each partner. Completing this stage establishes the first 2 × 2 structure.

Tetra X

The main expansion stage: four direct positions, with four positions under each. The reference illustrates the progression across the available levels without assigning an earnings amount.

Direct referral

A direct referral is a person you personally introduce into the network. The approved visual distinguishes direct referrals from positions that become available further down the structure.

Next position

These are positions available within the structure after the direct referral layer. Placement and qualification remain subject to the applicable platform rules.
