Understanding the Earning Structure

Potential rewards should be understood in the context of the approved Double X → Tetra X referral journey and the conditions that apply.

Income Disclosure

EleotLink does not guarantee any income, earnings, or financial returns. The diagrams and examples on this site explain structure and participation only. Any potential reward depends on the official programme rules, qualified participation, network activity and the conditions applicable at the time.

Structure → Reward Context

How the Approved Journey Relates to Rewards

The earning page should follow the same structure a member sees on the 1-9-90 page. It does not replace the official programme rules or assign a guaranteed payout to any position.

Double XFirst stage · 2 × 2 referral structure

First-stage completion

Completing the Double X structure establishes the first approved stage of the referral journey. If the official programme rules attach any reward, eligibility is determined by those rules—not by the illustration itself.

Tetra XMain stage · 1 × 4 expansion

Main-stage progression

The Tetra X stage expands the direct layer to four positions and shows four positions under each partner. Any reward eligibility associated with progression is subject to the applicable qualification, activity and placement rules.

Qualified activityParticipation and network conditions

Qualification matters

Structure alone is not a payout trigger. Where rewards exist, eligibility may depend on activation, qualified referrals, completed positions, active status, timing and other programme conditions.

Important: No reward amount, percentage or personal-income projection is implied by the Double X or Tetra X diagrams. Use the official programme terms and onboarding information for the applicable reward rules.
Before Any Reward

Participation Requirements

Potential rewards, where applicable, depend on meeting the conditions published by the programme.

  • Active Membership: Your account must be activated and remain in the required standing.
  • Qualified Participation: Referrals and positions must meet the applicable qualification rules.
  • Structure Conditions: Any stage or position requirement must be satisfied as defined by the official programme.
  • Verified Payout Method: Where required, a valid and verified payment method must be available.
  • Compliance: Participation must follow the platform terms, applicable rules and local requirements.
Reward Conditions

What Can Affect Reward Eligibility?

The following are structural considerations, not guaranteed payout triggers.

Stage completion

Completion of an approved stage may be relevant where the official programme rules specify a stage-based qualification.

Qualified referrals

A direct referral may need to complete activation or other qualification steps before it counts toward an applicable condition.

Position completion

The available positions shown in Double X and Tetra X illustrate structure. Eligibility may depend on how those positions are actually filled and qualified.

Active status and timing

Some programmes apply active-status, timing, cycle or other conditions. The official rules determine whether and when any reward is payable.

Illustrative Example

Illustrative example — not a guarantee of earnings

This example says nothing about the amount, timing or certainty of any reward; those outcomes depend on the official programme rules and actual qualification.

The exact reward amounts, percentages and conditions should be confirmed through the official programme information before participation.
