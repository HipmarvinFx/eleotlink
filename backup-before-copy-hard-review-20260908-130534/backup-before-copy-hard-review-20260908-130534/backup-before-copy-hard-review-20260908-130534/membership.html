Membership

Joining EleotLink

Everything you need to know before becoming a member. No surprises, no hidden terms.

Eligibility & Requirements

  • Legal Age: You must be of legal age in your country or jurisdiction to enter into a membership agreement.
  • Valid Contact: An active email address and WhatsApp number are required for communication, verification, and support.
  • Accurate Information: All registration details must be truthful. False information may result in account suspension.
  • Jurisdiction: You are responsible for ensuring that participation in referral networking platforms is legal in your location.
  • Sponsor: Membership typically requires a referral from an existing member. If you do not have one, contact support for placement.

Activation & Fees

Activation Fee

A one-time activation fee may be required to join the network. Registration and activation are completed through the official 1-9-90 platform, where the applicable amount and cryptocurrency payment instructions are displayed before you proceed.

Cryptocurrency Payment

Registration and activation payments are made exclusively through cryptocurrency using the payment instructions provided by the official 1-9-90 platform. EleotLink does not process membership payments.

Membership Duration

Membership duration and any applicable renewal conditions are determined by the official 1-9-90 platform and should be reviewed during registration.

Renewal

Any renewal requirements and their effect on participation or reward eligibility are determined by the official 1-9-90 platform and should be reviewed before registration.
