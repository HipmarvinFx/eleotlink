Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about EleotLink before you join.

EleotLink is a referral and networking platform built around the 1-9-90 organisational structure. Members connect, refer others, and participate in a defined network framework. It is not a job, investment vehicle, or guaranteed income programme.

The 1-9-90 model is an organisational framework with three tiers: 1 core leader, 9 active referrers, and 90 community members. It describes how the network is shaped, not a guaranteed formula for income. See our 1-9-90 Structure page for full details.

Members join through a sponsor and are placed within a network unit. As they refer others, the network grows through the 1-9-90 tiers. Rewards, where applicable, are triggered by specific network conditions such as tier completion or cycle rules.

You can join by contacting an existing member for a referral link, or by reaching out to EleotLink support directly. You will complete registration, review the terms, and pay the activation fee to activate your membership.

The activation fee and any renewal fees are displayed during registration before you are asked to pay. There are no hidden charges. See our Membership page for details on fees, duration, and refund policy.

As a member, you receive a referral link or code. When someone uses it to register and activate, they become part of your network. Placement follows the 1-9-90 rules, including spillover when tiers are full.

Rewards are triggered only when specific conditions are met — such as tier completion, cycle completion, or qualified referral thresholds. All conditions are explained during onboarding. Rewards are not guaranteed.
