Privacy Policy

How EleotLink handles information submitted through its website contact channels.

Information We Collect

When you contact EleotLink through the website, we may receive the information you choose to submit, including your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, enquiry topic, message, and consent record.

How We Use It

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, provide support, answer questions about membership and the 1-9-90 structure, and communicate with you about your request.

Form Processing

The website contact form is submitted through Web3Forms. Information entered into the form is transmitted to the service so that the enquiry can be delivered to the configured EleotLink receiving email.

Your Choices

You may contact EleotLink through the Contact page if you have questions about information you have submitted or wish to request clarification about its handling. Please do not submit information that is not necessary for your enquiry.
