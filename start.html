What exactly is 1-9-90?

1-9-90 is the referral-based opportunity introduced through EleotLink. It uses a structured network model in which participants introduce others and may receive rewards according to the applicable rules and conditions.

The purpose of this page is simple: help you understand before you decide. You can review the structure, participation requirements, reward conditions and important disclosures before choosing whether to take the next step.
