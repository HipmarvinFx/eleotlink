Rewards

Understanding the Earning Structure

Clear information about participation requirements, potential rewards, and what conditions apply.

Income Disclosure

EleotLink does not guarantee any income, earnings, or financial returns. The majority of participants in referral-based structures do not earn significant rewards. Any potential benefit depends entirely on individual effort, network growth, qualified referrals, and the specific rules of the 1-9-90 structure. This page contains illustrative examples only. They are not promises of personal results.

Participation Requirements

Before any reward can be considered, the following conditions must be met.

  • Active Membership: Your account must be activated and in good standing.
  • Qualified Referrals: Referred members must complete activation and remain active.
  • Structure Completion: Some rewards require specific tiers to reach defined occupancy.
  • Verified Payout Method: You must have a valid, confirmed payment method on file.
  • Compliance: You must adhere to all platform rules, terms, and ethical referral practices.

Potential Reward Triggers

Rewards are not automatic. They are triggered only when specific network conditions are met.

Tier Completion Rewards

When a defined tier (e.g., the 9 or 90) reaches required occupancy, a reward may be distributed to eligible participants in that branch.

Cycle Rewards

Some structures operate on a "cycle" basis. When a network unit completes a full cycle, rewards may be distributed according to the cycle rules.

Referral Bonuses

Direct referral bonuses may apply when you personally introduce a new member who completes activation. Rules vary by tier and promotion period.

Reinvestment

Some reward structures require or encourage reinvestment to maintain position or unlock higher tiers. Reinvestment rules are disclosed before commitment.

Illustrative Example

Illustrative example — not a guarantee of earnings

For illustration, a completed 1-9-90 network unit represents 1 core position, 9 active positions, and 90 community positions — 100 positions in total. Reaching any defined reward condition depends on the actual participation, qualification, and activity requirements of the applicable rules. No reward amount is assumed in this example, and no part of the illustration represents a promise of personal results.

The exact reward amounts, percentages, and conditions are provided during onboarding and are subject to change. No projection on this page constitutes a promise of personal results.
