About

What Is EleotLink?

A straightforward platform for referral networking, built on structure and transparency.

Our Purpose

EleotLink exists to provide a structured, transparent environment for referral-based networking. We believe that when people understand exactly how a platform works — including its limitations — they can make informed decisions about participation.

The platform is built around the 1-9-90 organisational model, a tiered framework that distributes participants across three levels. Our goal is to make this structure easy to understand, easy to join, and operated with full disclosure of terms, conditions, and reward rules.

We do not promise financial freedom. We do not guarantee income. We do present the opportunity clearly, explain the requirements honestly, and let you decide if it fits your goals.

What We Stand For

Transparency

We publish our structure, terms, and reward rules openly. Nothing is hidden behind a paywall or revealed only after payment.

Integrity

We do not manufacture testimonials, inflate numbers, or present illustrative examples as typical results.

Community

We foster a network where members support each other, share knowledge, and grow together within the structure rules.

Accountability

We maintain clear terms, a privacy policy, and accessible support channels. We answer questions directly.
