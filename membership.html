Membership

Joining EleotLink

Everything you need to know before becoming a member. No surprises, no hidden terms.

Eligibility & Requirements

  • Legal Age: You must be of legal age in your country or jurisdiction to enter into a membership agreement.
  • Valid Contact: An active email address and WhatsApp number are required for communication, verification, and support.
  • Accurate Information: All registration details must be truthful. False information may result in account suspension.
  • Jurisdiction: You are responsible for ensuring that participation in referral networking platforms is legal in your location.
  • Sponsor: Membership typically requires a referral from an existing member. If you do not have one, contact support for placement.

Activation & Fees

Activation Fee

A one-time activation fee is required to join the network. The exact amount and currency are displayed during registration before any payment is collected.

Payment Methods

We accept payments via Paystack, Flutterwave, and other verified local payment processors. Cryptocurrency options, if available, are clearly labelled.

Membership Duration

Membership is valid for a defined period (e.g., one year). The exact duration is confirmed before payment. You will receive renewal reminders before expiry.

Renewal

Renewal is required to maintain active status and reward eligibility. Renewal fees, if applicable, are disclosed in advance. Failure to renew may result in position forfeiture.

Cancellation & Refunds

You may cancel your membership at any time by contacting support via email or WhatsApp. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not entitle you to a refund of fees already paid.

Activation fees are generally non-refundable once payment is confirmed and your position is activated. In exceptional cases (e.g., technical error during payment), a refund request may be reviewed within 7 days of activation.

Upon cancellation, your position in the network may be deactivated or reassigned according to the platform's rules. Any pending rewards may be forfeited. Rejoining typically requires a new activation fee.

Memberships are non-transferable. Each account is tied to the individual who registered. Attempting to sell or transfer an account is a violation of terms.
