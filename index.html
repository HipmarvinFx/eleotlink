What Is EleotLink?

A Referral Network Built on Structure

EleotLink is a participation platform where members connect, refer others, and grow their network within a defined organisational framework. It is not a job, investment, or guaranteed income programme.

The Framework

The 1-9-90 Structure

The model is organised into three participant tiers. It explains network shape, not a promise of specific returns.

1

Core Leader

The top position in each network unit. Responsible for network coordination, support, and maintaining engagement within the structure.

9

Active Referrers

The engaged layer directly connected to the core. They introduce new participants and help expand the network reach.

90

Community Base

The broader participant foundation. This tier represents the wider reach and engagement potential of the network.

The exact business meaning of each tier, including reward triggers and conditions, is confirmed during membership onboarding. This visual is explanatory only.
