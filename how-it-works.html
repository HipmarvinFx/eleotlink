Member Journey

How EleotLink Works

A simple, transparent process from joining to participating in the network.

5 Steps to Participation

Every step reflects the actual operating rules of the EleotLink platform.

1

Join

Create an EleotLink membership through the registration process. Provide accurate contact information and review the membership terms before activating.

2

Participate

Understand the 1-9-90 structure, your position within it, and the rules that govern network growth and reward eligibility.

3

Refer

Introduce eligible participants to the network. Each qualified referral contributes to the structure according to the placement rules.

4

Network Growth

As your referrals join and participate, the network expands. Growth is organic and depends on the activity of all participants in your branch.

5

Rewards

Where applicable, members may receive rewards according to the published rules, conditions, and their position within the structure. Not guaranteed.
